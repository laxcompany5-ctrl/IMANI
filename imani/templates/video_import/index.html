Import a video

Direct MP4/WebM/MOV URLs work out of the box. Platform pages (YouTube, Vimeo, etc.) need yt-dlp installed. Only download footage you have permission to use.

The source video is kept intact. Snapshots are stored in instance/video_imports/<id>/snapshots/original/ and …/snapshots/4k/. Download limit: {{ max_mb }} MiB.

4K copies are created with a high-quality Lanczos upscale and black padding to preserve the aspect ratio. Upscaling adds pixels; it cannot recover detail that was never in the source. True enhancement needs an AI super-resolution model, which is not bundled.

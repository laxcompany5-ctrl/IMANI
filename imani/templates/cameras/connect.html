What happens after scanning

  1. A new Phone device is registered automatically and appears on the Connected Devices page.
  2. The phone asks for camera (and microphone) permission and starts streaming at about 2 frames per second.
  3. IMANI records continuous {{ config.VIDEO_SEGMENT_SECONDS|int }}s video segments and extracts snapshots every {{ config.SNAPSHOT_INTERVAL_SECONDS|int }}s.
  4. Snapshots are checked for exposure, contrast and sharpness before saving. Accepted frames get shadow lift / highlight recovery and are stored in the Snapshots library.
  5. Recordings and snapshots are indexed for AI search, just like CCTV feeds.

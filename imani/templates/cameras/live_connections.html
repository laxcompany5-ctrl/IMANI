How Coordination Works

  • Surveillance Manager - Coordinates all camera monitors, each running in its own thread
  • Frame Processing - Agent/vision pipeline extracts traits (height, build, posture, gait) from frames
  • Auto-Capture - Snapshots saved every ~30s from active feeds to instance/snapshots/
  • Video Recording - Frame buffers saved as reference videos to instance/videos/
  • Detection & Matching - Captures ingested to matching engine, scored against individual register with AI weights
  • Archive - Detection-triggered clips saved with timestamps to instance/media/
  • AI Search - All media indexed and searchable by description or photo upload

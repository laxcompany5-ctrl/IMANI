What happens after scanning

  1. The phone opens the capture page for {{ camera.name }} — no sign-in needed.
  2. Tap Start camera and allow camera (and microphone, if offered) access.
  3. Frames are posted to IMANI at about 2 frames per second; while a phone is streaming, it becomes this camera's live feed.
  4. Audio is uploaded in 10-second slices. Snapshots save automatically every {{ config.SNAPSHOT_INTERVAL_SECONDS|int }} seconds and video is recorded in {{ config.VIDEO_SEGMENT_SECONDS|int }} second segments.

Both devices must be on the same network and port {{ request.host.split(':')[-1] if ':' in request.host else '80' }} must be reachable through the firewall.

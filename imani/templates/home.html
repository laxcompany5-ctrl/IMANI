Security first

Designed for sensitive work

IMANI treats identity data as sensitive by default, at every layer.

  • Werkzeug scrypt password hashing
  • CSRF protection on every state-changing request
  • Brute-force lockout after repeated failures
  • HTTPS-only, HttpOnly & SameSite sessions
  • Candidates ranked with an explicit confidence band
  • Google sign-in via OAuth 2.0 + PKCE
